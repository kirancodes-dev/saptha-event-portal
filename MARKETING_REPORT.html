Saptha Event Portal

The AI-Powered Ecosystem for Academic & Cultural Event Orchestration

Executive Summary: The Saptha Event Portal is not just a registration system, but a comprehensive event lifecycle platform. By integrating advanced AI matching, automated narrative reporting, and a robust multi-tenant architecture, it transforms event coordination from a manual administrative burden into a data-driven strategic operation.

🚀 Unique Selling Propositions (USPs)

🧠 AI-Driven Judge Matching Semantic analysis of judge expertise vs. project descriptions to ensure the highest quality of academic evaluation.
✍️ Automated Narrative Reports Transforms raw event statistics and registration data into human-readable success stories for institutional reporting.
🏢 Multi-Tenant Architecture Supports independent operations for different clubs and departments under a single administrative umbrella.
⚡ End-to-End Lifecycle Seamless flow from event discovery → registration → check-in → judging → certification.
🛠️ Industrial-Grade Tech Stack Built with Python/Flask, PostgreSQL, and Firebase, ensuring scalability, security, and high availability.
📱 Omnichannel Access PWA support ensures a native-app feel on mobile devices for coordinators and participants.

🛠️ Technology Highlights

  • Intelligence Layer: Integration of Google Gemini for semantic matching and content generation.
  • Performance Layer: Celery & Redis for asynchronous processing (Bulk emails, AI analysis).
  • Security Layer: Implementation of JWT, OAuth, 2FA, and security-hardened headers.
  • Database Layer: Hybrid persistence model utilizing the power of both NoSQL (Firestore) and Relational (Postgres) data.
