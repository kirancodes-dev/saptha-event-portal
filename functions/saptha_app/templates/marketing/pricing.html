Frequently Asked Questions

Can we migrate from another university portal?

Yes. SapthaEvent features automated CSV mapping tools to import candidate registrations, scoreheets, and event history directly.

How does tenant billing function?

Pro accounts are billed annually. Stripe checkout processes all transactions globally, with Razorpay integration specifically for Indian colleges.
