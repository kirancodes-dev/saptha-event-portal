Platform Overview

Everything You Need to Power Your Fests

A modern, scalable suite of tools engineered specifically for college event administration and student engagement.

Multi-Tenant SaaS Architecture

Each university gets an isolated database namespace, custom branding configurations, and dedicated hostnames or subdomains.

Smart Waitlists & Promos

Automated waitlists promote candidates instantly upon cancellation. Multi-tiered discount systems and custom coupons boost velocity.

Gemini AI Integrations

Leverage Google Gemini AI for smart scheduling conflicts, student recommendation matching, judge pairing, and automated form generation.

QR Tickets & Offline PWA

Generate secure PDF tickets with encrypted QR codes. The offline-ready Progressive Web App permits check-in scan matching at fest gates.

Real-Time Analytics

A unified analytics portal detailing registrations, finances, category distribution, and attendance rates with export capabilities.

Secure Compliance Suite

Advanced GDPR tools, audit log trails, masked PII values, database backups, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) keep your university secure.
