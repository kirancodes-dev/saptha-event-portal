This notice explains how SapthaEvent handles your personal data, with reference to the Information Technology Act, 2008 and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India.

1. Information We Collect

When you use SapthaEvent, we collect:

  • Identity data: Full name, email address, phone number, University Serial Number (USN)
  • Event data: Event registrations and the answers you give on their forms, attendance records, scores, feedback you send, certificates
  • Payment data: Transaction IDs (processed securely via Razorpay — we do not store card details)
  • Usage data: Login timestamps, IP addresses (for security audit logs only)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Registering you for events and generating your entry ticket
  • Sending confirmation emails, QR tickets, and certificates
  • Notifying you via WhatsApp (if you provide your number)
  • Generating academic achievement records for the university
  • Fraud prevention and security monitoring

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

3. Data Sharing

Your data may be shared with:

  • Sapthagiri NPS University — for academic records and event coordination
  • Google Cloud — hosts the portal, its database and its files
  • Razorpay — payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Brevo and Google (Gmail) — deliver the portal's emails
  • Meta (WhatsApp) or Twilio — deliver WhatsApp messages, only where the university has switched them on and you have agreed to receive them
  • Google (Sign in with Google, Gemini) — if you choose to sign in with Google; and, where the AI features are switched on, the text a feature works on (for example a question you ask the assistant)
  • Government authorities — if required by law

4. Data Retention

Registration and attendance data is retained for 5 years as required for academic records. You may request deletion of your personal account data, subject to legal retention requirements.

From your profile page you can download your data (your profile, registrations, form answers, feedback, certificates, notifications and consent) and delete your account. A deletion takes effect after 30 days (you can cancel until then): your login is removed; your name, email, phone, USN and form answers are erased from every registration; and your name and comments are removed from feedback you sent. Attendance and payment totals stay for the records, without your details. Certificates already issued in your name, payment records and the security audit log are kept.

5. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

  • Right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal data
  • Right to erasure (where not required for legal/academic purposes)
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India

6. Cookies & Sessions

We use a server-side session cookie (session) to keep you logged in. This cookie does not track you across other websites. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used.

7. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never as the password itself. Connections are TLS-encrypted (HTTPS). Your session is kept on the server; the cookie holds only its identifier. Repeated failed sign-ins are slowed down, and administrative actions are recorded in an audit log.
