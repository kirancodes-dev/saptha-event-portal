This policy is issued in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2008 and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India.

1. Information We Collect

When you use SapthaEvent, we collect:

  • Identity data: Full name, email address, phone number, University Serial Number (USN)
  • Event data: Event registrations, attendance records, scores, certificates
  • Payment data: Transaction IDs (processed securely via Razorpay — we do not store card details)
  • Usage data: Login timestamps, IP addresses (for security audit logs only)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Registering you for events and generating your entry ticket
  • Sending confirmation emails, QR tickets, and certificates
  • Notifying you via WhatsApp (if you provide your number)
  • Generating academic achievement records for the university
  • Fraud prevention and security monitoring

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

3. Data Sharing

Your data may be shared with:

  • Sapthagiri NPS University — for academic records and event coordination
  • Google Firebase/Firestore — our database provider (data stored in India)
  • Razorpay — payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Brevo / Twilio — email and WhatsApp delivery services
  • Government authorities — if required by law

4. Data Retention

Registration and attendance data is retained for 5 years as required for academic records. You may request deletion of your personal account data, subject to legal retention requirements.

From your profile page you can download all your data and delete your account. A deletion takes effect after 30 days (you can cancel until then): your login is removed, and your name, email, phone, USN and form answers are erased from every registration. Attendance and payment totals stay for the records, without your details.

5. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

  • Right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal data
  • Right to erasure (where not required for legal/academic purposes)
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India

We use a server-side session cookie (session) to keep you logged in. This cookie does not track you across other websites. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed using bcrypt/scrypt. All connections are TLS-encrypted (HTTPS). Session data is stored server-side in Redis. We run regular security audits.
