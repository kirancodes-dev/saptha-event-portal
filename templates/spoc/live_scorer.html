How to score this match {% if state.mode == 'pkl' %}
    {% if (state.mat and state.mat.named) or (squads.home|length >= live.MAT and squads.away|length >= live.MAT) %} {#- With line-ups the scorer taps the players (UPG-417, UPG-420) #}
  1. Before the start: tap each team's seven who start and press Save line-ups. Choose who raids first (won the toss), then Start 1st half.
  2. Every raid is three taps. The blue box over the players always says which step you're on.
    1. Step 1: tap the raider, the player who goes in, on the blue (raiding) side. He turns blue and says RAIDER; the public sees him at once.
    2. Step 2: tap each defender he touched (green side), or the one who tackled him. Each turns amber and says TOUCHED. Nobody touched? Skip this step.
    3. Step 3: tap one big button for how the raid ended. Touch points (he touched them and got back), Tackled (they caught him), Bonus only, Empty raid or Raider out of court. Tick He also crossed the bonus line first if he did.
  3. The page says Saved: and the raid. The portal puts players out, brings them back in order, counts all outs and do-or-die, and gives the next raid to the other team.
  4. Other (under each team): Substitution (tap it, the player going off, then the substitute), Out of bounds (tap it, then the player who stepped out), −1 on the mat.
    5. {% else %}
  5. Choose who raids first (the team that won the toss), then press Start 1st half.
  6. After every raid, pick the raider{% if jerseys is defined %} (by jersey number){% endif %} and, if tackled, the tackler. Then tap one button for how the raid ended: the number of touches, Bonus only, Empty raid, Tackled or Raider out of court. Tick + Bonus first if the raider also crossed the bonus line.
  7. The portal adds the points, all outs, revivals and do-or-die by itself, and passes the raid to the other team.
    8. {% endif %}
  8. A time-out, technical point or review: open Calls. After a time-out press Resume clock.
  9. A wrong tap: Undo last, or Remove next to it under All points.
  10. Half time, then Start 2nd half. At the end, Full time: save the result. A level score goes to the tie-breaker: 5 raids each.
{% else %}
  1. Press Start 1st half.
  2. For every point, tap the button on the side of the team that got the points. Pick the player in Who scored first if you know who.
  3. A raid with touches and the bonus is two taps, for example Raid +2 then Bonus +1. An all out is the raid's points, then All out +2.
  4. A time-out or review: Pause clock, then Resume clock.
  5. A wrong tap: Undo last, or Remove next to it under All points.
  6. Half time, then Start 2nd half. At the end, Full time: save the result.
{% endif %}
