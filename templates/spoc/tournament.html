Something went wrong? How to fix it here
  • A wrong score: open the match's "Enter / Update Result" and save the right one. If the knockout's next match is already played, clear that result first ("Fix this match").
  • Full time pressed too early on the phone: open "Continue live scoring" and press "Reopen the match", or "Clear the result" here.
  • A point given to the wrong team or player: on the scoring page, press Remove next to it in "All points".
  • A team didn't turn up: "Fix this match" → Give walkover. A team pulling out of the tournament: Withdraw it in "Teams in the fixtures".
  • Wrong team name, wrong team in a slot, a late team: "Teams in the fixtures" (rename, replace, add) or "Fix this match" → Set the teams.
  • Wrong points rule: League standings → Change the points rule. Playoffs made too early: Undo the playoffs.
  • Results published with a mistake: correct the match, then "Publish the corrected results".
